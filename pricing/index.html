Questions

Do you take a cut of what my clients pay me?

No. SeeGlance never holds or processes payments between you and your clients. Your invoice links point to your own Payoneer, Wise, Skrill or crypto account. The only money we handle is the Pro subscription.

Who processes the Pro subscription payment?

Paddle.com is our Merchant of Record. Paddle takes the payment, handles sales tax and sends your receipt. Cards and other payment methods available in your region are shown at checkout.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Email support@seeglance.com and we will cancel it. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Any charge can be refunded within 30 days, see the Refund Policy.

Do I need a US bank account?

No. That is the point of SeeGlance. You choose how you get paid, and we link to it.
