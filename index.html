Questions

Before you sign up.

The things people actually ask us — not the polished FAQ template version.

Do you hold my money?
No. SeeGlance never touches, holds, or processes funds. Every invoice links out to a payout method you already control — Payoneer, Wise, or a crypto wallet. Your client pays you directly through that method; we just generate the invoice and track whether it's been paid.
Why not just send my client a Payoneer or Wise link directly, without a tool like this?
You can — plenty of freelancers do exactly that today. SeeGlance exists for what happens around the payment link: the proposal, the signed contract, a professional client portal, and reminders when an invoice goes quiet — instead of stitching that together across Google Docs, Canva, and a WhatsApp thread.
How is this different from HoneyBook or Dubsado?
Same category of tool, different starting assumption. HoneyBook and Dubsado are built around a US or Canadian bank account processing your payments. SeeGlance is built for freelancers who don't have one — you keep whatever payout method already works for you, and we never require a US EIN or Canadian Business Number to sign up.
What about taxes — does SeeGlance help with that?
SeeGlance isn't a tax tool and won't file anything for you. What it does do: every invoice keeps a clean, exportable record of what was billed and paid, so you're not reconstructing income from bank statements and chat threads when it's time to declare.
What will it cost?
Pro is $9/month, with founding-member pricing locked in for early sign-ups. The core hub — proposals, contracts, invoices, and the client portal — also has a free tier. Early access members get a say in what we build next.
When can I actually use it?
