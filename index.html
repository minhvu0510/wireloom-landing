Questions

Before you sign up.

The things people actually ask us — not the polished FAQ template version.

Do you hold my money?
No. Wireloom never touches, holds, or processes funds. Every invoice links out to a payout method you already control — Payoneer, Wise, or a crypto wallet. Your client pays you directly through that method; we just generate the invoice and track whether it's been paid.
Why not just send my client a Payoneer or Wise link directly, without a tool like this?
You can — plenty of freelancers do exactly that today. Wireloom exists for what happens around the payment link: the proposal, the signed contract, a professional client portal, and reminders when an invoice goes quiet — instead of stitching that together across Google Docs, Canva, and a WhatsApp thread.
How is this different from HoneyBook or Dubsado?
Same category of tool, different starting assumption. HoneyBook and Dubsado are built around a US or Canadian bank account processing your payments. Wireloom is built for freelancers who don't have one — you keep whatever payout method already works for you, and we never require a US EIN or Canadian Business Number to sign up.
What about taxes — does Wireloom help with that?
Wireloom isn't a tax tool and won't file anything for you. What it does do: every invoice keeps a clean, exportable record of what was billed and paid, so you're not reconstructing income from bank statements and chat threads when it's time to declare.
What will it cost?
We're targeting $12–15/month, with founding-member pricing locked in for early sign-ups before public launch. Final pricing isn't set in stone yet — early access members get a say in that.
