Refund Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

30-day money-back guarantee. If SeeGlance Pro isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of any charge and you'll get your money back, no hard questions.

How to request a refund

  • Email support@seeglance.com from the address on your account, or contact Paddle directly at paddle.net.
  • Include the email you used at checkout so we can find the order.

How refunds are processed

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders, so refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method. The time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer, and is usually a few business days. When a refund is granted, Pro access ends and your account returns to the Free plan.

Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time by emailing support@seeglance.com. If you cancel without a refund, you keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again. Your data stays available on the Free plan.

Your statutory rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the consumer-protection laws where you live, such as a right to withdraw from a purchase within a set period. Paddle's Checkout Buyer Terms also apply to your order.

Free plan and invite codes

The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund. Invite or promo codes have no cash value and can't be exchanged for money.
